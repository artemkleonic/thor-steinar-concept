# Product context

Independent, unofficial Thor Steinar shop redesign concept for a Ukrainian developer's business presentation. Not commissioned by the brand, no affiliation, no public deployment authorized.

The audience is existing German-speaking brand customers and the brand's management. Visitors must recognize actual Thor Steinar garments and their original logos, find clothes by category/audience/search, inspect a product, keep favorites and try a clearly marked demo basket. The owner wants premium dark fashion presentation, not a generic unbranded outdoor store.

User-approved requirements: deep black #0B0C0E, graphite #17191C, slate #24272B, chalk #F2F2F0, restrained accents; large confident photography and typography; German interface; responsive widths 1920/1440/1024/768/390/375; sticky header; categories Jacken/Hoodies/T-Shirts/Accessoires; featured products; details and collection sections; full footer. Real brand clothing, logos, product names and photographs from the official site are now explicitly requested. Generated models must wear reference garments and be marked as AI concept imagery. No political/military symbols as decoration.

Preserve React + Vite static hosting, search, local favorites, validated size selection, demo basket quantities/removal, keyboard focus/escape behavior, meaningful empty states, reduced motion. No backend, payments, registration, fabricated forms, prices, availability, reviews, awards or brand history. Product facts need source URLs. Text can combine short verified phrases with original German paraphrases. Keep rights/provenance documented and avoid presenting this as an official campaign.

The user explicitly authorized autonomous implementation and asked not to repeat discovery questions. This context records the supplied brief and subsequent corrections; it introduces no new product claims.
