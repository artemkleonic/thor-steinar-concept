# Design direction / The Northern Edit

## Review of the first version

- The hero and core shopping interactions are a useful foundation.
- Four identical category tiles repeat the product-grid layout.
- The same campaign photo repeats in three large sections.
- Numbered labels suggest a sequence where there is none.
- Secondary copy and small mobile targets need stronger readability.
- Source was concentrated in one component and compact CSS overrides.

## Design system

- Ink `#0B0C0E`: page and navigation.
- Graphite `#17191C`: editorial surfaces and sheets.
- Slate `#24272B`: boundaries and quieter surfaces.
- Chalk `#F2F2F0`: headings and primary actions.
- Steel `#A6ABB2`: readable secondary text.
- Oxblood `#A7463C`: restrained state accents.

Barlow Condensed Bold carries the campaign headlines and typographic wordmark. Arial carries product information and controls. Headings remain strong and uppercase where the original fashion brief requires it; instructional and descriptive copy uses ordinary German sentence case.

## Layout

Left-aligned campaign typography against a coastal image opens the page. Categories use a large outerwear image beside three compact product entrances. The catalogue remains a predictable four-column grid, two on mobile. A close-up textile photograph separates catalogue browsing from the second campaign, which introduces a different model and composition.

Desktop: Hero → asymmetric category story → functional product grid → material detail → secondary collection campaign → official-shop handoff.

Mobile: Hero → one prominent jacket category → compact vertical category list → two-column catalogue → stacked editorial block → collection campaign.

## Review against the brief

Dark palette, condensed headlines and product photographs are retained because they are explicitly requested. Generic section numbering and repeated card layouts are removed. Photography is fictional and labelled; no origin story, certification, material specification, pricing or stock claims are invented. No original brand imagery was present in the starting source. The official site is referenced for category naming and navigation only.

Motion is concentrated in the initial hero and visitor-triggered controls; content is available immediately without section reveal effects. Reduced motion is supported. Search, favourites, product size selection and the demo basket remain the primary interactive tests.
