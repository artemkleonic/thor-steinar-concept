# Thor Steinar / brand-grounded redesign

## Direction

Retain the approved dark northern fashion world and replace its anonymous clothes with recognizable real products. The strong moment is a campaign portrait tied directly to EIKIN II. Product cutouts remain unaltered official photographs; generated lifestyle frames are disclosed separately. The emblem belongs on the actual garment and original brand mark, never as a background ornament.

## Tokens

Ink #0B0C0E; raised #17191C; line #34383F; chalk #F2F2F0; muted #B5BBC3; accent #A7463C; photograph surface #F2F2F0. Font roles: self-hosted Barlow Condensed 700 for display, self-hosted Barlow 400/500/600 for information. Body 16px; supporting text at least 12px on mobile; display up to 96px. Spacing 8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96.

## Composition

Hero portrait and one dominant collection action; compact useful category selection; six real products in a 3-column desktop / 2-column mobile catalogue; one close-up of an actual print; a lighter women's campaign tied to SUPPORT; quiet service footer. Keep category labels out of photographs. No decorative section numbering or heading eyebrows. Favor readable descriptive product content over repeated prototype terminology.

## Interaction

Navigation updates real category/audience/new-product filters. Product cards show original photos, actual model names and links to exact source products. The demo basket has no prices or checkout and says so clearly. Native modal with visible close, Tab containment, Escape and focus return. Useful inline pressed/selected states and confirmations. No animation except a restrained hero entry and user-triggered controls; reduced-motion removes travel while preserving visible state.

## Responsive

Hero copy sits beside the subject on desktop and below the visible face on mobile. Mobile categories stay compact so the catalogue arrives earlier. 44px control targets wherever practical, fluid headings, no image text overlap, no horizontal scrolling. Verify all six requested widths and actual product-name lengths.

## Provenance

Official original assets and exact product URLs are recorded in docs/brand-sources. AI requests, references and results are recorded separately. These choices are authorized for this private local concept; publication is out of scope.
